Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept

AeroVoxel modifications: base-color/branding and emissive textures removed,
normal, ambient-occlusion and roughness detail maps retained with their UV transforms,
geometry retained, orientation/scale normalized for the tunnel, and display materials
customized. Original model copyright metadata is retained.
The upstream asset credits Khronos logos (2015) and 3D Commerce logos (2017);
those marks are not used to imply endorsement. The original model/texture source
was optimized by the asset authors from Unity Fan's public-domain concept car.

This asset illustrates a demo object. Flow is computed on its projected 2D
silhouette; it is not a full 3D CFD result.
